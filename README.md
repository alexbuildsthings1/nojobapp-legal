# NO JOB — support and legal

Public pages that go with the **NO JOB** app (App Store name *NO JOB: Layoff Day Counter*).

- [Support](https://alexbuildsthings1.github.io/nojobapp-legal/) — the App Store "Support URL" (this site's root)
- [Privacy Policy](https://alexbuildsthings1.github.io/nojobapp-legal/privacy_policy.html) — the App Store "Privacy Policy URL"
- [Terms of Use](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) — Apple's Standard EULA

NO JOB counts every day since you got laid off, live on your Home Screen, Lock Screen,
Dynamic Island and Apple Watch, until the day you get hired. It has no account, no
backend, no analytics and no ads — the timer stays on your devices. The only data that
leaves the device is a pseudonymous purchase record (a random ID, not linked to you; Apple + RevenueCat) if you buy
"Unlock all themes".
