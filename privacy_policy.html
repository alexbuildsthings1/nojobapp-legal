Summary. The NO JOB app does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. Your timer is stored only on your devices. If you buy the optional Unlock all themes purchase, Apple processes the payment and our purchase service, RevenueCat, keeps a pseudonymous record of it (a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account) so it can be checked and restored (see Section 9). The only other personal data the Developer ever receives is what you choose to send us by email when you contact support. That email is received and stored in a mailbox hosted by Google Workspace, so it is processed by Google on our behalf (see Section 8).

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application NO JOB (also shown as "No Job"; the "App"), including its widgets, Live Activity and Apple Watch app, and its developer handle information. The App is published by Oleksandr Syrota (the "Developer", "we", "us" or "our"). This policy applies to the App on iPhone and Apple Watch and to any email correspondence you have with us about the App. By installing or using the App, or by contacting us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with it, do not use the App and uninstall it.

2. Contact details

For app support and for privacy or data-protection requests (including requests to exercise your rights under Section 13), email nojobapp-support@codeandsprites.online. This mailbox is hosted on Google Workspace.

3. What the App does not do

The App is designed to work on your devices, without an account. In particular, the App:

  • does not ask you to create an account or to sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, employer, or any other identifier;
  • does not include any analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK, and does not collect usage data or diagnostics of its own;
  • does not display advertising and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • does not track you across apps or websites;
  • does not access your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or health data;
  • does not transmit your timer, its dates or your settings to the Developer or to any server operated by the Developer.

The Developer operates no backend service for the App and has no technical means to access your timer.

4. Your timer is stored on your devices only

The App stores the following in its private storage area on your iPhone: the date and time your timer started; the date and time you stopped it, if you did; the start and stop dates of earlier timers you stopped; your display settings (display format, seconds, theme); and whether you have unlocked the paid themes. A copy of what the widgets need is kept in a storage area shared only between the App and its own widgets on the same iPhone.

Widgets, Lock Screen and Live Activity. If you add NO JOB widgets or allow its Live Activity, your day count, start date and the day's line are shown on your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Dynamic Island, and some of these places are visible to anyone who can see your iPhone, even when it is locked. The count can reveal that you are between jobs. Only add these widgets where you are comfortable with others seeing them; you can remove them at any time.

Apple Watch. If you install the Apple Watch app, the iPhone sends the timer, the selected theme's colors and your language directly to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This data travels between your own devices and is not sent to the Developer.

The App doesn't add its own encryption; it relies on the encryption and file protection built into iOS and watchOS. Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device, to a device backup, or to your device's file system may be able to read it. You are solely responsible for securing your devices (for example with a passcode or biometric lock) and for any backups you create. Your iPhone's backups (iCloud or computer) may include this data according to your own backup settings, which are provided by Apple and governed by Apple's terms.

Resetting the timer, or uninstalling the App, deletes this data from your device. The App offers no account, cloud sync or export of its own, and the Developer cannot recover deleted or lost data.

5. Sharing your timer

The App can make an image of your timer (the "share card") on your device. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share. When you do, the image and a short message, including a link to the App on the App Store, are handed to the iOS share sheet, and the message is also copied to your clipboard. Where the image and message go next is your choice, and what happens to them there is governed by the app, service or person you share them with. The Developer does not receive a copy. Please think before you share: the card shows how long you have been counting and can show your start date.

6. Device permissions

The App may ask iOS for the following permissions. Each is optional and is used only for the purpose described. Denying a permission only disables the related feature.

  • Add to Photos — only when you choose Save Image for a share card. This permission lets the App add images to your library; it cannot see, read or upload your existing photos.
  • Live Activities — to show your running timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The Live Activity is updated on the device itself; no push-notification server is involved. You can turn it off in the iPhone Settings app.

7. Contacting us by email (support requests)

Contacting us is entirely optional and is never required to use the App. If you choose to email us, including through Settings → Contact support in the App (which pre-fills the App's version number in the subject), we receive and process:

  • your email address and the name shown on your email account;
  • the content of your message and of any replies, and any attachments or screenshots you include;
  • any other information you decide to include, such as your device model, operating-system version, App version, or purchase details;
  • technical email metadata that is added automatically by email systems, such as the date and time of sending and message headers (which may include IP addresses of mail servers).

Purposes. We use this information only to read and answer your request, to investigate the problem you report, to handle any privacy or data-protection request you make, and to keep a record of our correspondence in case of a later question or legal claim. We do not use it for marketing, we do not add you to any mailing list, we do not sell or rent it, and we do not combine it with any other data.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR). We process support emails on the basis of our legitimate interests in answering the messages we receive and in keeping appropriate records (Article 6(1)(f)); where your email relates to your purchase, on the basis that it is necessary to respond to you in connection with that contract (Article 6(1)(b)); and where you exercise a data-protection right, to comply with our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Please send only what is needed. Do not include passwords, payment card details, health information, or details about your former employer or your job search in an email. We do not ask for such information and do not need it to help you. Anything you choose to include is sent at your own discretion and at your own risk.

9. Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat

The App is free to use. The optional, one-time Unlock all themes purchase is not a subscription.

  • Apple. The purchase is made and paid for through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system, under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The Developer does not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or billing address. Apple provides the Developer with sales reports and, only if you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, aggregated usage and crash information, as described in Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • RevenueCat. To check, restore and keep track of the purchase, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States; the Purchases SDK). When the App starts or you buy or restore, the App connects to RevenueCat, which receives a random app-user identifier created by the SDK (pseudonymous: it isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account), your device's identifier for vendor (IDFV, an ID iOS gives the apps of one developer on your device), the App Store transaction information for the purchase (product, date, store country and currency), and technical information needed to provide the service, such as the device model, the App and iOS versions, the device's language and region, and the IP address of the connection. RevenueCat uses it to validate and restore your purchase, and gives the Developer access to the purchase record and to aggregated purchase statistics (for example, how many unlocks were sold).

This purchase record is not linked to your identity: it contains no name, email address or Apple Account, and the Developer has no means of telling who made a purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold. RevenueCat acts as our data processor (service provider) under its data processing terms, and may process the data in the United States; such transfers are protected by the safeguards RevenueCat provides under applicable law, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See RevenueCat's privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy) for details. We process the purchase record because it is necessary to provide the purchase you made (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for aggregated statistics, on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding sales of the App (Article 6(1)(f)).

If you never make or restore a purchase, the App may still connect to RevenueCat to load the price and check whether there is a purchase to restore; no personal information about you is sent beyond what is described above.

10. Other network activity and links

Apart from Section 9, the App makes no network requests to the Developer or to third parties, except as follows. Each of these is governed by the privacy policy of the respective third party, not by this policy.

  • Terms of Use. When you open the Terms of Use, the App first checks that Apple's page loads, then opens it from apple.com in an in-app browser. Apple receives the standard information of any web request, such as your IP address.
  • App Store, Mail and other links. "Rate the app" opens the App's page in the App Store at the "Write a Review" step; "Contact support" opens your email app. The App hands these links to the apps that open them and does not gather or attach any information about you or your device. Anything you post on the App Store is submitted to Apple under its own terms, may be publicly visible together with your store nickname, and is stored and moderated by Apple, not by the Developer. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a review.

The Developer does not control these third parties and is not responsible for their data practices.

11. Data retention and deletion

  • Your timer lives only on your devices and is under your control. You can reset it in the App, or delete everything by uninstalling the App from your iPhone (and its Watch app). The Developer holds no copy.
  • The purchase record at RevenueCat is kept while the Developer uses RevenueCat to provide the purchase, so that it can be restored, and is otherwise deleted according to RevenueCat's retention rules. Because it is pseudonymous and not linked to your identity, we can only act on a request about it if you can give us identifying details of the transaction (for example, the order ID from Apple's purchase receipt).
  • Support emails are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in Section 7 — to handle your request, to refer back to it if you contact us again, and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims — or for longer where required by law. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time (Section 13).
  • Store reviews are retained and can be edited or deleted according to the rules of the App Store.

12. Sensitive information

The App does not ask why you are counting or who you worked for, and it has no free-text fields. Still, the fact that you use it, and the dates in it, can suggest that you lost your job. That information stays on your devices (Section 4) unless you choose to share it (Section 5) or send it to us (Section 7). The Developer accepts no responsibility and no liability, to the fullest extent permitted by law, for the disclosure of information that you choose to display, share or send.

13. Your rights

Apart from the support emails and the pseudonymous purchase record described in Sections 7–9, the Developer holds no personal data about you. To exercise any right you have in relation to that data, email nojobapp-support@codeandsprites.online from the address you used to contact us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the time required by the law that applies to your request. We may refuse requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive, or that the applicable law does not require us to fulfil.

  • EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP): the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority — in particular in the country where you live or work (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). We would, however, appreciate the chance to address your concern first.
  • Other countries: you may have rights under your local law, and we will honour them only to the extent that law applies to us.

We do not sell or share personal information, including for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

14. Children

The App is not intended for or directed at children. Anyone under the age of majority in their country must not use the App, make a purchase in it, or email us without the permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for their use of the App. If we learn that we have received personal data from a child without such permission, we will delete it.

15. Security

The App relies on the security features of your devices and their operating systems. The App's connections to RevenueCat and Apple use encryption in transit (HTTPS). Support emails are protected by the security measures of Google Workspace and by access controls on our account. Email is transmitted using encryption in transit (TLS) where your own email provider supports it, but standard email is not end-to-end encrypted, and no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. The Developer makes no guarantee that the App, your devices, or any email system is free from vulnerabilities and, to the fullest extent permitted by law, accepts no liability for unauthorized access to, or loss of, data stored on your devices or information you choose to send by email. You send any information by email entirely at your own risk.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds new features or if we change a service provider. The current version is always bundled inside the App under Settings → Privacy policy, and published on this website, together with its effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the updated policy.

17. Contact

For help with the App, questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise your data-protection rights, email nojobapp-support@codeandsprites.online. Emails are processed by Google as described in Section 8.
